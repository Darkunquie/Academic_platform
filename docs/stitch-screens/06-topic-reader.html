01 — TOPIC TENSES

Tenses, in three breaths.

To master a language is to master time itself. In English, tenses are not merely grammatical rules; they are the architectural scaffolding of narrative. They define when an action occurred, how long it lasted, and its relationship to the present moment of speaking.

The Present Contemplation

The simple present is often a misnomer. It rarely describes what is happening "now" (that is the domain of the present continuous), but rather what is true eternally. Habits, universal truths, and scheduled futures find their home here.

format_quote
"Language is the road map of a culture. It tells you where its people come from and where they are going."

The Temporal Arc

Consider the shift from "I wrote" to "I have written." One is a finished artifact in the graveyard of the past; the other is a living connection between what was and what is.
